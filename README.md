# Hexagon Chaos Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 30, 2026**

This policy covers the Hexagon Chaos puzzle game, developed by Md Mozibur Rahman, on these platforms:

- **Hexagon Chaos for Windows**, available from the Microsoft Store
- **Hexagon Chaos for Apple devices**, including iPad and Mac, available from the App Store, where it may also be listed as Hexagonal Chaos

## Summary

Hexagon Chaos does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your game progress stays on your device, and purchases are handled entirely by Microsoft or Apple.

## Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The game has no sign-in and does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. Nothing about you or the way you play is sent to us.

## Information stored on your device

The game saves a small amount of information on your device so that it works the way you left it:

- your best times
- your chosen difficulty
- how many free puzzles you have played today
- your sound setting and whether you have seen the first-run tips

This information never leaves your device through the game and we cannot see it. Your device's own backup service, such as iCloud Backup, may include it in your personal backups, under that service's terms. Uninstalling the game removes it from your device.

## Subscriptions and payments

Hexagon Chaos offers optional Premium subscriptions. Purchases are processed by the Microsoft Store on Windows and by the App Store on Apple devices. We never receive or store your payment details or your account information.

The game only asks the store whether your subscription is active, so that it can unlock Premium. The store handles your purchase under its own privacy policy:

- [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement)
- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Internet access

The game plays without an internet connection. It only goes online to reach the Microsoft Store or the App Store, to show prices and to buy, restore or check a subscription.

## Advertising, analytics and tracking

The game contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking of any kind. We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.

## Information from Microsoft and Apple

Like all app developers, we may see summary statistics from Microsoft and Apple, such as download and sales totals. If you have allowed your device to share diagnostics, they may also give us anonymous crash reports. We use these only to improve the game, and they do not identify you to us.

## Children's privacy

Hexagon Chaos is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as the right to access or delete it. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove the information stored on your device, uninstall the game. For purchase data, contact Microsoft or Apple. You can also contact us with any question.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page and the effective date above will be updated. Because we do not collect contact details, we cannot notify you directly, so please check this page from time to time.
